## **Module 1: The Web Crawler – Harvesting Knowledge**
Every great journey starts with a quest for knowledge. Our first tool, the 
**Web Crawler** (a.k.a. "lil spyder"), is designed to scrape Wikipedia pages, 
summarize their content, and store it in a neat `.json` format. Why? So you 
can train specialized LLMs on distilled, high-quality data. Think of it as 
building your own digital library—one that’s efficient, structured, and ready 
for AI consumption.

### **What We Built**
- **Start**: A simple crawler that fetches URLs and saves HTML as compressed 
JSON. - **Challenges Overcome**:
  - Thread safety issues (fixed with locks and a thread pool). - Storage 
  limits (handled with graceful exits and compression). - Domain restrictions 
  (ensured we stayed within Wikipedia).
- **Final Form**: A multi-threaded crawler that respects depth limits, cleans 
data, and summarizes sections for easy LLM ingestion.

### **Key Insights**
- **.json vs. PDF**: JSON wins for efficiency and ease of parsing—perfect for 
feeding LLMs. - **Summarization**: We simulated summaries (e.g., 100 words) 
but left room for real LLM integration later.

**Artifact**: `mylilspyder_2.py` – Your very own web scraper, ready to crawl!

---

## **Module 2: Network Mapping – The Holmes Investigation**
Knowledge is power, but so is knowing your own turf. Enter **Network Spyder**, 
your tool for mapping the local network (`192.0.2.0/24`). Inspired by 
Sherlock Holmes, this script pings devices, grabs MAC addresses, scans ports, 
and logs everything in JSON. It’s your digital magnifying glass.

### **What We Built**
- **Ping Sweep**: Finds active devices (e.g., your Arch machine at 
`192.0.2.109`, a printer at `192.0.2.103`). - **Port Scanning**: Uses 
`nmap` to reveal open ports (e.g., `80`, `443`, `9100` on the printer). - 
**MAC Lookup**: Identifies device vendors (e.g., identified from the MAC prefix).

### **Key Insights**
- **Printer Mystery**: Open ports (`515`, `631`, `9100`) screamed 
“printer”—confirmed as a network printer. - **Cellphone Hunt**: Devices like 
`192.0.2.105` (no ports, mobile MAC) were likely candidates. - **Security**: 
Open ports on your machine (`80`) flagged as potential risks.

**Artifact**: `network_spyder.py` – Your network detective, always on the 
case.

---

## **Module 3: System Health – Dr. Watson’s Diagnosis**
With the clues gathered, it’s time for **Dr. Watson** to step in. Our 
`system_doctor.py` script analyzes the master report, flags vulnerabilities 
(e.g., outdated packages, open ports), and teaches you how to fix them. It’s 
your personal system health assistant.

### **What We Built**
- **Master Report**: Compiles hardware, software, and network data into 
`.json` and `.txt`. - **Diagnosis**: Flags issues like open ports on your 
machine or high packet activity. - **Education**: Provides commands to fix 
problems (e.g., `sudo systemctl stop httpd` to close port `80`).

### **Key Insights**
- **Apache on your host**: A web server running on your machine—Watson suggests 
securing it. - **Printer Ports**: Normal for printers but could be 
exploited—Watson advises router restrictions. - **Updates**: Outdated 
packages? Watson says `sudo <your package manager> update`.

**Artifact**: `system_doctor.py` – Your system’s health advisor, always ready 
with a remedy.

---

## **Module 4: AI Integration – Watson Goes Digital**
Why stop at scripts when you can have an AI narrate your system’s health? We 
integrated **Ollama**, a local LLM, to act as Dr. Watson himself. Feed it the 
master report, and it returns a formal, investigative analysis—complete with 
recommendations and a touch of Victorian flair.

### **What We Built**
- **Ollama API**: Sends `master_report.json` to Ollama (e.g., 
`llama3.2:latest`). - **Prompt**: “You are Dr. Watson. Analyze this report and 
narrate your findings.” - **Output**: Watson’s voice in `doctor_report.txt`, 
diagnosing issues and suggesting fixes.

### **Key Insights**
- **AI Narration**: Watson might say, “Holmes, port 80 on our machine is a 
vulnerability—shall we secure it?” - **Local Power**: No cloud needed—your AI 
runs on your machine, analyzing your data.

**Artifact**: `system_doctor.py with Ollama` – Your AI assistant, channeling 
Dr. Watson.

## **Module 5: Automation – Cron Jobs for Vigilance**
System awareness isn’t a one-time thing—it’s a habit. We set up **cron jobs** 
to keep your tools running automatically, ensuring your system stays secure 
and up-to-date.

### **What We Built**
- **Daily**: Network scan, master report, and Watson’s diagnosis. - 
**Hourly**: Packet capture with `tcpdump` for network monitoring. - 
**Weekly**: Software updates and dependency checks. - **Every 6 Hours**: AI 
analysis with Ollama.

**Artifact**: Cron job commands – Your automated security schedule.
