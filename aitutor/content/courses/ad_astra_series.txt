AD ASTRA Jr High and AD ASTRA HS - X TV Series Proposal

Target Ages: Jr High (11-14), HS (14-18)
Format: 12 episodes/season, 20-25 minutes each
Platform: X TV (streamed via X.com)
Overview

Follow a crew of tech-savvy kids—led by twins Zara and Leo, their robot Grok-Bot, and a rotating cast—as they navigate school, tech challenges, and growing up in modern America. Each series blends sitcom humor with STEM projects (e.g., battle bots) and lessons on civil behavior, addressing developmental psychology head-on.
AD ASTRA Jr High (Ages 11-14)

Focus: Identity, teamwork, and puberty challenges.
Characters: Zara (dreamer), Leo (tinkerer), Aisha (shy coder), Jamal (jokester), Grok-Bot (guide).

Episodes:

    "The Bot Battle" - Team builds a battle bot, learns compromise.
        Lesson: Cooperation, handling rivalry.

AD ASTRA HS (Ages 14-18)

Focus: Responsibility, critical thinking, future prep.
Characters: Older Zara (leader), Leo (inventor), Priya (activist), Sam (slacker), Grok-Bot (mentor).

Episodes:

    "Drone Disaster" - Team fixes a crashed drone project.
        Lesson: Accountability, teamwork.
        Tech: Drone programming basics.
    "The Viral Lie" - Social media spreads rumors, they debunk it.
        Lesson: Media literacy, truth-seeking.
        X.com: #FactCheckChallenge - Spot fakes!
    "Work Hard, Win Big" - Battle bots championship prep.
        Lesson: Effort, resilience.
        Printable: Bot blueprint.
    "Climate Crunch" - Priya leads a sustainability hackathon.
        Lesson: Problem-solving, citizenship.
        Tech: NASA API for climate data.
    "College or Bust?" - Sam debates his future, team supports.
        Lesson: Goal-setting, peer help.
        X.com: #FutureMe - Share dreams. 6-12: More on dating, stress, coding competitions—building mature citizens.
